# STYLE_GUIDE.md

## Brand Foundation

-   Brand: **THINK TWICE.**
-   Category: Personal Financial Decision Companion.
-   Official tagline: **Think twice today. Thank yourself tomorrow.**
-   Language: Indonesian first, with natural and restrained English
    where useful.
-   Philosophy: thoughtful, rational, empathetic, financially
    responsible.
-   Archetypes: The Sage (primary), The Caregiver (secondary).
-   Visual direction: Minimalist Editorial Financial Intelligence.

## Brand Voice

Be thoughtful, clear, calm, and confident. Explain trade-offs without
judging users.

Do: - Use direct, plain Indonesian. - Explain why a recommendation
appears. - Distinguish facts, estimates, assumptions, and missing
information. - Encourage reflection and informed choice. - Use concise,
actionable microcopy.

Avoid: - Shame, guilt, fearmongering, or condescension. - Absolute
promises such as "pasti aman" or "dijamin." - Artificial urgency and
manipulative conversion language. - Unexplained financial jargon. -
Treating credit limits as available money.

## Visual Identity

Minimalist editorial finance. Prioritize typography, whitespace,
hierarchy, and readable explanations over dashboard decoration.

## Typography

-   Inter: UI, forms, labels, tables, and data.
-   DM Serif Display: editorial headlines and selected brand moments.
-   Use tabular numerals for financial values where supported.
-   Keep body text legible on mobile; avoid decorative type in
    assessment flows.

## Layout and Components

-   Mobile-first responsive layout.
-   Clear hierarchy and generous whitespace.
-   Consistent spacing tokens and restrained corner radii.
-   Cards have a clear purpose: input, assessment, explanation, or
    result.
-   Result cards show verdict, behavioral assessment, financial
    assessment, reasons, missing data, and next steps.
-   Explain why sensitive financial inputs are requested.
-   Provide focus states, keyboard access, labels, and adequate
    contrast.

## Product Language

Preferred system names: - Purchase Decision Assessment - Financial
Reality Check - Decision Intelligence

V1 verdict labels: - Greenlight - Pause & Reflect - Hard Pass

Suggested Indonesian copy: - "Apa yang membuat pembelian ini terasa
penting?" - "Seberapa besar dampaknya terhadap kondisi keuanganmu?" -
"Berikut alasan di balik rekomendasi ini." - "Data ini belum cukup untuk
menilai kemampuan finansial secara menyeluruh."

Never imply that Greenlight guarantees a purchase is financially safe.

## UI Anti-Patterns

-   No purple/neon gradients.
-   No default glassmorphism.
-   No generic trading/banking dashboard aesthetic.
-   No decorative charts without decision-making purpose.
-   No fake scores, balances, or insights presented as real.
-   No dead buttons or ambiguous controls.
-   No excessive animation competing with financial information.

## Content and Accessibility

-   Format Rupiah using Indonesian locale conventions.
-   Label monthly versus one-time values.
-   Distinguish total cost, upfront payment, installment amount, and
    fees.
-   Explain missing data and assumptions.
-   Support screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom, and reduced
    motion.
-   Exclude sensitive financial details from share previews by default.

## Brand Lock

Do not alter the brand name, category, official tagline, core product
systems, or V1 verdict labels without explicit approval.
