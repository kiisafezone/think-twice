# Sponsorship Policy

## Purpose

THINK TWICE. is an independently developed Personal Financial Decision Companion. Sponsorship helps support the time and resources required to maintain, improve, document, and responsibly develop the project.

## Voluntary support

- Sponsorship is optional and does not affect access to the project.
- Sponsors do not receive ownership, control, or privileged access to the product.
- A sponsorship is not a purchase of financial advice, a guarantee of product delivery, or an investment.

You can support ongoing development through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/kiisafezone).

## Independence and editorial integrity

Sponsorship must never influence:

- Purchase Decision Assessment questions, scoring, or behavioral interpretation.
- Financial calculations, affordability thresholds, or verdict logic.
- Greenlight, Pause & Reflect, or Hard Pass recommendations.
- Product roadmap decisions where doing so would compromise user interests.
- Security decisions, privacy safeguards, or the handling of user data.

No sponsor may pay to change a user's result, suppress an unfavorable assessment, promote a financial product inside a recommendation, or access private user information.

The product's decision engine remains deterministic, documented, and independently governed. Commercial relationships must not override its rules.

## Use of contributions

Contributions may support project-related costs and work, including:

- Development, maintenance, testing, and accessibility improvements.
- Infrastructure and necessary software services.
- Documentation, security, and privacy work.
- Other expenses directly related to responsible project development.

Support does not guarantee that a particular feature, issue, or request will be prioritized or delivered.

## Recognition

Sponsors may be acknowledged in the [Acknowledgements](#acknowledgements) section below with their permission. Recognition is optional; sponsors may remain anonymous or request removal of an acknowledgement.

Recognition does not imply endorsement of a sponsor's products or services by THINK TWICE.

## Transparency

The maintainer should describe material sponsorship relationships when they could reasonably be perceived as relevant to a product decision. Sponsorship does not grant sponsors access to non-public user data, private assessments, or internal security information.

## Changes

This policy may evolve as the project grows. Any change must preserve the independence of financial assessments and the interests and privacy of users.

## Acknowledgements

Thank you to everyone who chooses to support the project.

Sponsors are listed here only when they have agreed to be publicly acknowledged. No sponsor is currently listed.

If you sponsor the project and would like your name or organization included, please open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub. You may also choose to remain anonymous.
